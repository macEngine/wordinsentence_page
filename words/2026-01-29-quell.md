# 《纽约时报》每日一词：quell

## 📖 词汇卡片

![](https://blogimg.niuge6.com/i/5343958467500.png)
**quell** 英 /kwel/ 美 /kwel/

`托福` `专八` `GRE` `quell`

## 📰 今日例句

**quell** 是《纽约时报》（2026 年 1 月 29 日刊）标题为 "Iran on the Brink" 一文中出现的一个单词：

> What you are describing, Farnaz, is just horrific and inconceivable, I think, to a lot of people, just these scenes of absolute carnage all over Iran. And I wonder whether the brutality of this response is working for the regime in terms of actually **quelling** the protests or whether it perhaps is having an opposite effect on people and what they're willing to do in the face of all of this opposition.

**解析：**
从拼写上来看，quell 是一个简洁有力的单音节词，其原意是指"镇压、平息"，引申为"压制、制止"。

从词源上来看，quell 一词源自古英语 cwellan，意为"杀死、毁灭"，与德语 quälen（折磨）同源。在中古英语时期，这个词的含义从"杀死"逐渐演变为"制服、压制"。到了现代英语，quell 主要用于描述通过武力或权威手段平息骚乱、叛乱或强烈的情感。这个词带有强烈的压制性和强制性意味，常用于政治、军事和社会冲突的语境中。与 suppress（压制）相比，quell 更强调通过强力手段迅速而彻底地平息；与 subdue（制服）相比，quell 更侧重于消除反抗或骚动。在新闻报道中，quell 经常用来描述政府对抗议、暴动或叛乱的镇压行动，也可以用来形容平息恐惧、怀疑等强烈情绪。比如：

- 镇压叛乱 quell a rebellion
- 平息骚乱 quell the riots
- 消除恐惧 quell fears

那么回到上面《纽约时报》的句子，翻译成中文是：Farnaz，你所描述的情况对很多人来说简直是可怕和难以想象的，伊朗各地这些绝对的大屠杀场景。我想知道，这种残暴的回应对政权来说，在实际**镇压**抗议方面是否有效，还是说它可能对人们产生了相反的效果，影响了他们在面对所有这些压迫时愿意做什么。

## 🔄 近义词辨析

| 词汇 | 含义侧重 | 例句 |
|------|---------|------|
| **suppress** | 压制（强调持续压制） | suppress dissent |
| **subdue** | 制服（强调征服） | subdue the enemy |
| **crush** | 粉碎（强调彻底摧毁） | crush the uprising |
| **stifle** | 扼杀（强调窒息、阻止） | stifle opposition |

好了，关于 quell 今天就看到这里。最后送各位一句英国首相温斯顿·丘吉尔（Winston Churchill）在 1940 年向下议院发表的著名演讲中的名句共勉：

> "We shall not flag or fail. We shall go on to the end. We shall fight in France, we shall fight on the seas and oceans, we shall fight with growing confidence and growing strength in the air, we shall defend our island, whatever the cost may be. We shall fight on the beaches, we shall fight on the landing grounds, we shall fight in the fields and in the streets, we shall fight in the hills; we shall never surrender. And even if, which I do not for a moment believe, this island or a large part of it were subjugated and starving, then our Empire beyond the seas, armed and guarded by the British Fleet, would carry on the struggle, until, in God's good time, the New World, with all its power and might, steps forth to the rescue and the liberation of the old."
> 
> —— Winston Churchill, Speech to the House of Commons, June 4, 1940
